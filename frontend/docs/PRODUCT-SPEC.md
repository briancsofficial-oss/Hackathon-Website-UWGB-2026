# Smart Flow — Product Specification

## Audience and outcome
Business owners and finance teams across business sizes. The app helps them build a clearer view of financial health from manually entered information and, in future, imported documents and authorized bank connections.

## Design
Warm cream background (#F5EEE4), black action panels (#121212), warm-white cards (#FFFAF4), rounded corners, mobile-first responsiveness.

## Six onboarding phases
1. Business basics
2. Revenue
3. Expenses
4. Assets and debts
5. Import documents
6. Review and questions

## First-version dashboard
- Revenue/expense trends
- Simple cash flow estimate with explicit limitations
- Assets and liabilities when supplied
- Data source, reporting period, and freshness labels
- Missing-data prompts

## Deferred
Production authentication and storage, real file processing, OpenAI-based document extraction, Plaid, accounting integrations, and multi-user collaboration.
